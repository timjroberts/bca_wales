# Open Government Licence version 3.0 — reviewed snapshot

Reviewed: 13 August 2026  
Canonical terms: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

The licensed information may be copied, published, distributed, transmitted,
adapted and exploited commercially or non-commercially. Reusers must
acknowledge the source with any attribution statement specified by the
information provider, preserve notices, avoid implying official status or
endorsement, and avoid misleading use. Personal data, third-party rights,
logos, military insignia, identity documents and other listed exemptions are
not covered.

For this release the provider-specific attribution in the source registry is
mandatory and the registry's public-safety and transformation limits still
apply. This file records the reviewed terms used by the immutable release; the
canonical page remains authoritative.
