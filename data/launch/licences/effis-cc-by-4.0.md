# EFFIS data licence — reviewed snapshot

Reviewed: 13 August 2026  
EFFIS terms: https://forest-fire.emergency.copernicus.eu/about-effis/data-license  
CC BY 4.0 legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

EFFIS data may be shared and adapted under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
The launch must credit `European Union, Copernicus EFFIS`, link the licence,
indicate clipping or other changes, retain notices, and avoid implying
endorsement. The separately recorded EFFIS methodological limits still apply:
its perimeter and dates are a provisional provider product, not incident-
authority truth.

This file records the reviewed terms used by the immutable release; the linked
EFFIS terms and CC BY legal code remain authoritative.
