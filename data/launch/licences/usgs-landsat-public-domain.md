# USGS Landsat public-domain status — reviewed snapshot

Reviewed: 13 August 2026  
Canonical statement: https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/are-there-any-restrictions-use-or-redistribution-landsat-data

USGS-authored Landsat data are in the public domain and may be used and
redistributed without restriction. USGS requests acknowledgement; this release
uses `Landsat imagery courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey`. Separate
third-party material, trademarks and implied endorsement are not covered.

The Planetary Computer assets used for acquisition mirror the exact named USGS
Collection 2 Level-2 products. The release records the USGS product identity,
canonical unsigned mirror path and exact input checksum; its retained archive,
not a temporary access signature, is the reproduction source.
