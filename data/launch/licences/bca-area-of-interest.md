# BCA-area of interest publication authority

The canonical `Blorenge.geojson` geometry is a BCA-owned, chair-reviewed
working-area record. Blorenge Commoners Association authorises its publication
and transformation in the public Blorenge landscape explorer, with attribution
to Blorenge Commoners Association.

This authority does not turn the geometry into a legal, official, surveyed or
current boundary of Blorenge Common CL18. Derivatives must preserve that
limitation and identify the source version used.
