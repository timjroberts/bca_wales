# Copernicus Sentinel data terms — reviewed snapshot

Reviewed: 13 August 2026  
Canonical legal notice: https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice

Copernicus Sentinel data are available on a free, full and open basis. The
release may reproduce, distribute, communicate, adapt and combine the data. It
must identify the source and visibly state: `Contains modified Copernicus
Sentinel data 2025–2026`. No endorsement is implied. Provider notices,
technical limitations and third-party rights remain applicable.

The Earth Search COGs used here are byte-addressable mirrors of the named
Copernicus Collection-1 Level-2A products. The release records both the
Copernicus product identity and the mirror acquisition URL and retains exact
input checksums.
